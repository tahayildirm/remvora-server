# Code of conduct

Participate respectfully and professionally. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and disclosure of private credentials or customer information are unacceptable. Give actionable technical feedback and respect differing experience levels.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's enabled private reporting channel; do not post personal information in public issues. Maintainers must establish a monitored private contact before opening the project to outside contributors.
